Method: [Australian rules methodology](../methodology/australian-rules.md).

Each parameter has one of three statuses:

- **Enacted:** in force for this year under an Act or regulation.
- **Published indexed:** the ATO's (or, for the Age Pension, Services Australia's and DSS's) published
  result of statutory indexation.
- **Assumption:** not yet set for the year. The stated value is a project
  choice, and the reason is given.

A test keeps every parameter in the TOML file listed in this register.

## Scope

The register covers one Australian resident individual for the full income
year: single, with no dependants and entitled to the full tax-free threshold.
They hold ordinary taxable investments, a taxed accumulation super account
in a complying fund, and account-based pensions paid from age 60. From Age
Pension age they may receive the single Age Pension at normal (not
transitional) rates and the seniors and pensioners tax offset; 10 years'
Australian residence is assumed. Situations
outside this scope are rejected with `UnsupportedRule`; see the last section.

### Pension minimums

- **Full year:** the minimum is the 1 July balance times the factor for the
  member's age at 1 July.
- **Part year:** a pension commencing during the year uses its commencement
  balance and age, pro-rated by the days remaining (including the commencement
  day) over the days in the year.
- **Late start:** a pension commencing on or after 1 June needs no payment
  that year.
- **Rounding:** minimums are rounded to the nearest $10.

The pro-rata and rounding conventions reproduce the ATO's 2019-20 worked
example. The 1 June rule comes from consistent secondary sources (fund and
industry technical guides). The Schedule 7 text itself has not been re-read
for this checkpoint.

### Access interpretation

Access is unrestricted at 65. It is also unrestricted from retirement after
reaching preservation age. A person who stopped work earlier meets the
retirement condition at preservation age, provided they intend never to
work again; that intention is a scenario input. Benefits from a taxed fund
are tax free from age 60. For anyone born from 1 July 1964, preservation
age is 60, so every supported lawful access is tax free.

## Unsupported in this checkpoint (rejected, not approximated)

- **Tax offsets:** foreign income tax offsets above the $1,000 no-calculation
  limit.
- **Super contributions:** excess concessional or non-concessional
  contributions.
- **Retirement-phase limits:** excess transfer balance.
- **Super benefits:** payments before age 60, and untaxed elements. Defined
  benefit, transition-to-retirement and early-release cases also fall outside
  the taxed accumulation scope.
- **Not modelled:** couples and family thresholds, part-year residency, the
  low income super tax offset, the government co-contribution, transitional
  Age Pension rates, Rent Assistance and the Commonwealth Seniors Health
  Card.
